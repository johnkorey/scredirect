Description

iCloud, the cloud storage service from Apple, keeps your photos, files, and notes up to date across your iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Windows PC.

KEEP YOUR PHOTOS IN SYNC

  • Access your entire photo library on any device
  • New photos upload automatically from your iPhone
  • Download originals to your PC anytime
  • Share albums with family and friends

iCloud, the cloud storage service from Apple, keeps your photos, files, and notes up to date across your iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Windows PC.

KEEP YOUR PHOTOS IN SYNC

  • Access your entire photo library on any device
  • New photos upload automatically from your iPhone
  • Download originals to your PC anytime
  • Share albums with family and friends

STORE FILES IN ICLOUD DRIVE

  • Keep documents, downloads, and desktop files in one place
  • Access files from File Explorer on Windows
  • Get 5 GB of free storage with your Apple ID
  • Preview files without downloading them

SYNC ACROSS ALL YOUR DEVICES

  • Keep contacts, calendars, and bookmarks up to date
  • Changes appear everywhere automatically
  • Pick up where you left off on any device
  • Sign in once with your Apple ID

SHARE WITH FAMILY AND FRIENDS

  • Share files and folders with a link
  • Collaborate on notes and documents
  • Use Family Sharing for shared storage
  • Control who can view or edit

KEEP YOUR DATA PRIVATE

  • Protect your account with two-factor authentication
  • Trust encryption in transit and at rest
  • Enable Advanced Data Protection for end-to-end encryption

BACK UP AND RESTORE

  • Restore contacts, calendars, and bookmarks from earlier versions
  • Recover recently deleted files and photos

WORK WHERE YOU ARE

  • Access iCloud on Windows, iPhone, iPad, Mac, and the web
  • Keep everything in sync automatically
