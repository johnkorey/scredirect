FILL OUT FORMS AND SHARE SECURELY

  • Complete forms quickly with fillable fields
  • Share documents as secure, viewable links
  • Add your signature or initials in just a few clicks
  • Protect files with controls that prevent unwanted edits

GIVE AND GET FEEDBACK ON PDFS

  • Add comments, sticky notes, highlights, and annotations
  • Type directly on PDFs or draw with freehand tools
  • Share files for review and keep all feedback in one place
  • Track and manage comments to stay organized

WORK TOGETHER BETTER

  • Share PDFs with a single link for easy access and collaboration
  • Collect feedback from multiple reviewers in a single file
  • Reply to comments and keep conversations moving forward
  • Get notified when others view or comment

SIGN DOCUMENTS WITH CONFIDENCE

  • E-sign documents in a few seconds with a personalized signature
  • Trust industry-leading security and reliability

WORK WHERE YOU ARE

  • Access and manage files on desktop, web, and mobile
  • Keep your work in sync across devices

TRY OUT AI TOOLS TO WORK FASTER AND SMARTER

  • Get instant summaries of your documents
  • Ask questions and find key information quickly
  • Understand multiple files quickly
  • Share with coworkers and collaborators for smart collaboration
